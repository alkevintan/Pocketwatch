Pocketwatch is a touch-first client for YouTube on phones and tablets.

It is a fork of SmartTube, which is built exclusively for Android TV and is not
designed for handsets. Pocketwatch keeps SmartTube's playback and data layer and
replaces the TV interface with one built for touch: a bottom navigation bar,
responsive grids that adapt to screen width, comments on the video page, and a
player with gesture seeking and fullscreen on rotation.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by SmartTube, Google or YouTube.
